### The problem

Good products stall at the border. Selling abroad takes people who speak the buyer's language: one overseas sales rep costs about ₩50M a year, and covering 17 language markets would take 17 of them. For a five-person manufacturer that math never works, so the product stays home and the market rewards company size over product quality.

AI models are now available to everyone. What separates companies is whether the work actually moves onto agents, and that transition is what we build.

### What we build

**[Rinda](https://rinda.ai)** is an AI sales agent for exporters. Tell it what you make; it finds overseas buyers, writes to each one in their language, runs the follow-up sequence and brings every reply into one inbox. More than 1,100 exporters use it.

**[Ara](https://arator.rinda.ai)** is an AI employee that works where your team already talks, from Slack to KakaoTalk. Give it real work and get finished files back, with every step logged; any action that sends, deletes or pays can be held for a person to approve.

**[TradeGraph](https://tradegraph.rinda.ai/?lang=en)** gives the AI you already use the export context a consumer brand needs: where to sell first, which buyers reply, how many follow-ups to send and what the shelf price will be. A hosted MCP server and REST API, free with no sign-up ([connection guides](https://github.com/FINGU-GRINDA/tradegraph)).

**[claude-rinda-plugin](https://github.com/FINGU-GRINDA/claude-rinda-plugin)** puts Rinda inside Claude Code and any MCP client: buyer search, enrichment, campaigns and replies in plain language. MIT licensed.

### How we work

**Agents do the repetition, people make the judgment calls.** Every tool an agent can touch is a switch: on, off, or behind a human review.

**A good demo impresses for five minutes. A good agent works every morning.** That is the bar for what we ship.

**We are our own first customer.** We sell Rinda with Rinda, and Ara works on our team before it works on yours. We hold ourselves to the rule we sell: grow output, not headcount.

**Delete before you automate.** Question the requirement, delete, simplify, speed up, and only then automate. A feature that does not serve the core job does not ship; internally we call this the kimchi fried rice rule.
